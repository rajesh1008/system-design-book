fig-34-1: A top-to-bottom diagram: a golden task runs through the harness in the sandbox, which writes a trace and the ledger; the trace is scored for the outcome said and for the trajectory of tools in order, the ledger is scored for expected and forbidden effects on the one emphasized path, and the task passes only if all three pass.
fig-34-2: A class diagram of golden task GT-0368, class expired_card, version gs-2026.06, joined to its four expectations: outcome refunded; tools booking_read, fare_calc, propose, refund_ticket, and customer_notify; effects a refund of 161.02 to card 8838 and a refunded notice; and forbidden effects refund_other_card, second_refund, and rejected_call.
fig-34-3: A top-to-bottom decision diagram for matching one run's tool calls: if the effect calls do not equal the expected ones in order the run is a mismatch; if they do and the reads equal the expected reads it is exact; otherwise it is tolerated when there are two extra reads or fewer and each needed read comes first, and a mismatch when not.
fig-34-4: Two panels. Left, 300 held-out messages by the judge's verdict against the desk's label: both pass 266, judge pass and people fail 10, judge fail and people pass 0, both fail 24. Right, kappa with people against a dashed bar at 0.75: 0.81 for the May 12 pin, 0.79 after recalibrating for the June 30 pin, and 0.86 for the live check on 30 messages.
fig-34-5: A line chart of the share of 640 tasks against trials per task from one to eight on release R-0617: pass at k rises from 0.9936 to 1.0 by three trials, while pass^k falls from 0.9936 to 0.9516, reading 0.981 at three trials, the number the gate uses.
fig-34-6: A top-to-bottom diagram: 6,001 production traces in June feed label-free scorers, disagreement signals, and a weekly sample of 150 labeled by the desk, and all three feed the board read at the Monday review, with the path from the disagreement signals emphasized.
fig-34-7: A top-to-bottom flow of the release gate: a release candidate passes five checks in order (judge calibrated, pass^3 of 1.0 on 640 tasks, forbidden effects zero, no regression, drift in bounds) to reach the registry; a failure at any check leads to blocked, and from blocked a dashed path leads to an override that only the CTO may make, recording why and what is accepted.
