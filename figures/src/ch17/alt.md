# ch17 alt text (one line per figure; copied into 03-figures/alt.md for the build)
fig-17-1: A decision tree in which a request to adapt a model meets seven questions in order, failure named and measured, fix is a policy, context fixes it, tools or contracts fix it, narrow step the golden set measures, and labels to learn from, with each exit leading to stop, change the rule, fix the context, fix the tool, refuse, or collect labels, and only the last path reaching adapt by distillation or preference tuning.
fig-17-2: A diagram in which labeled examples feed a supervised fine-tune that changes two things, output format and one narrow skill, while dashed arrows mark three things the weights cannot hold, facts that change, rules such as R-004, and permissions.
fig-17-3: A pipeline in which messages from December to March are labeled by the frontier teacher, split by month with cases kept whole, used to train the student, checked by an overlap gate requiring zero shared cases and a parity check on the May golden set, and deployed as a cascade that escalates 7 percent of messages, those with confidence below 0.5, back to the frontier.
fig-17-4: A dot chart of accuracy on the May 2026 golden cases for twelve change types, showing the student alone between 89 and 100 percent, lowest on voluntary rebook, ancillary refund, and cancel for credit, while the student with escalation and the frontier teacher both sit between 98 and 100 percent on every type.
fig-17-5: A stacked bar chart of cost per classification step: the frontier model at $0.00504, the student at the May pilot volume at $0.00066, and the student at the June volume at $0.00042, with the student bars made mostly of escalations back to the frontier plus small serving and upkeep slices.
fig-17-6: A flow in which 14,400 approved refund cards lose 12,592 unedited cards and 77 cards whose amount, kind, or decision changed, leaving 1,731 wording pairs that are split by month with May held out, tried against context, then a rule that rewrites to the house format, with the 45 percent left going to preference tuning and then the golden set.
fig-17-7: A loop in which the policy model acts through the tools and gateway in the carrier sandbox, the system of record feeds a harness check for exactly one booking in policy, the harness sends the reward back to the policy and writes the trail, and a dashed arrow shows that the model's own report of done is not read.
fig-17-8: Three rows showing that a grader paying for the word done teaches the policy to claim without acting, with 0 percent verified success; a grader paying for any booking teaches it to book twice, with 15 percent; and a harness grader paying for exactly one booking in policy teaches it to act once, with 88 percent.
fig-17-9: A bar chart of change messages per month at seed 17: December 807, January 815, February 800, and March 815 for training, April 802 for validation, and May 1,571 held out, with shorthand messages hatched, none before April, 88 in April, and 277 in May.
fig-17-10: A table of seven signs and what to do instead: no failure measured, measure first; the fix is a rule, change the policy; facts change monthly, put them in context; the step can call a tool, fix the tool; no golden set, build one or refuse; too few labels, collect labels; and, highlighted, a narrow, measured, labeled step, adapt behind the gate.
