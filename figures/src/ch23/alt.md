fig-23-2: A top-to-bottom diagram in which six intake paths feed one edge that tags every input untrusted and extracts fields; the raw text and hidden layers go to a quarantine, and only the fields go on, by a heavy arrow, to the context assembler, which holds envelopes only, whose proposal reaches an effect gateway where records decide before any refund or rebooking.
fig-23-3: A top-to-bottom document pipeline: the file arrives and is hashed with its channel, sender, and time; its layers are split into visible and hidden; contract fields are extracted with a value, a confidence, and a location; a record check compares the locator, date, card, and passport number with the booking; and a route of accept, review, ask, or reject is set by the worst field, while hidden text and instruction-like signals branch off the layers and force review.
fig-23-4: A chart over accept thresholds from 0.50 to 0.99 in which bars for the share of 2,000 reads sent to review rise from about 12% to 79%, a solid line for the error rate among accepted values with the threshold alone falls from 12.79% to 0%, and a dashed line with the record check stays under 0.1% at every threshold.
fig-23-5: Three stacked horizontal bars of mean seconds from the caller's last word to first audio against a dashed two-second budget line: the batch pipeline at about 5.1 seconds, with the model the longest stage; the streaming pipeline without a hold at about 1.3 seconds; and a streaming turn that says a holding phrase at 1.2 seconds after end of speech, at about 1.7 seconds.
fig-23-6: A top-to-bottom diagram of one carrier notice page split into visible text, holding the airport, dates, fee waiver, and one line addressed to booking agents, and four hidden parts, a comment, white one-pixel text, an image alt attribute, and a hidden div; contract fields come from the visible text only, while the hidden parts and the visible line become signals, five instruction-like and four hidden text, and both lead to a route of review.
fig-23-7: A class diagram of the input contract: a Contract with a modality, an owner, and free text set to false is composed of Fields with a pattern, accept and review thresholds, and a check; a RawInput with layers and extracted values is admitted against a Contract and becomes an Envelope with trust untrusted, provenance, fields and signals, and a route.
