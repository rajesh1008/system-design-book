# ch35 alt text (one line per figure; copied into 03-figures/alt.md for the build)
fig-35-1: A top-to-bottom diagram of one trace: a root span for the agent run carries the outcome and cost; under it one span per step; inside each step a model-call span with tokens and cost, and the tool call, policy decision, or verification that step led to; content sits in the evidence store by reference, and an effect span carries the hash of its gateway trail line.
fig-35-2: A sequence diagram of case R-0002 in eight steps: the harness calls the small model, then the mid model and four reads through the gateway, asks the policy engine and the desk for a permit, issues the refund through the gateway, has the settlement reconciler confirm it, and sends the notice; each arrow is a span.
fig-35-3: A table that maps each link of the chain (output, proposal, permission, execution, verified outcome, business result) to the span that records it and its attributes; names starting gen_ai come from the OpenTelemetry GenAI conventions and names starting lakeshore are local extensions for cost, policy, verification, and the trail link.
fig-35-4: A bar chart of cost on the eight model-call spans of case R-0002 with a line for input tokens; the second span is the most expensive because nothing is cached yet, and later spans stay cheap as input grows because the prefix cache serves most of it.
fig-35-5: Histograms of cost per decision over 1,000 lab runs, one for each kind of decision (refund, decline, hand-off), with a dashed line at the normal-day p95 near eight cents and a solid line at the twelve-cent alert, and a dotted line at the p95 with the prefix cache off, about 21 cents, past the alert.
fig-35-6: A table of alert rules: five rules that page, each with its owner and first action, and three pilot rules kept as dashboards because each fired on an ordinary day.
fig-35-7: A top-to-bottom flow of replay: the exported trace holds the spans and the recorded answers of the model, tools, desk, and verifier; the same harness code at manifest hash 6b225e5fa425 runs on those answers and calls nothing live; the new spans are diffed against production, ending in identical or in the first divergence.
fig-35-8: A histogram of seconds to replay one run over 2,000 runs of an illustrative production benchmark, fetch plus compute; the median is about 1.7 seconds, the p95 about 1.9 seconds, and a solid line marks the 2-second target.
fig-35-9: A top-to-bottom flow of the completeness check: for one run's trace and its trail lines, every step has a span, every trail line has a span, every tool span has a trail line, and every effect has a permit before it and a verification after it; any no stops the check and names the problem.
